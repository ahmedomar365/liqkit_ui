`LiqToolbar` is a 44pt horizontal bar of `LiqToolbarGlassButton`s or
`LiqToolbarChip`s. Leading items are pinned to the start; trailing items are
pinned to the end with a `Spacer` in between.

## Actions

A toolbar with glass pill buttons — use for primary navigation actions such
as Back and Done.
