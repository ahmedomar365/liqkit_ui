`LiqRadio` is the iOS 26 radio circle. It is a 22pt ring that animates
in a centered 12pt filled dot when its `value` matches the surrounding
`groupValue`. The visible ring sits inside a 44pt tap target for
comfortable hit testing and renders at 40% opacity when disabled.

## Single

Three free-standing `LiqRadio` widgets sharing a single selection
state. Tap any one to make it the active value.

## Group

`LiqRadioGroup` lays out a vertical list of `(value, label)` pairs as
tappable rows. Tapping anywhere on a row selects that row's value.
