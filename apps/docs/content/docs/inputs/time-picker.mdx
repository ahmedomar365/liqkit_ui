`LiqTimePicker` is the iOS 26 3-column scroll-wheel time picker. Each
column is an infinite-scrolling wheel that snaps to the nearest tick;
the centered row is framed by a pair of hairline dividers.

## 12-hour

The default. Three wheels — hours (1-12), minutes (00-59), and AM/PM —
laid out side-by-side. Hours and minutes loop infinitely; AM/PM is a
finite 2-entry wheel.

## 24-hour

Set `use24HourFormat: true` to render hours 0-23 and hide the AM/PM
column. The picker collapses to a 2-column layout.

<LiqPreview component="time-picker" variant="24-hour" />

<CodeSnippet snippet={timePicker24HourSnippet} />

## Intervals

Pass `minuteInterval` to step the minute wheel by larger increments.
The value must evenly divide 60 (e.g. 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30). Initial
minute values are snapped down to the nearest tick.
