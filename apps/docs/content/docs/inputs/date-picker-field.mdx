`LiqDatePickerField` is the shadcn/ui-style date picker — a 44pt-tall
pill trigger that displays the formatted date (or a placeholder) and
opens a `LiqCalendar` in a popover beneath the field when tapped.
Selecting a day commits the new value through `onChanged` and closes
the popover; clicking outside dismisses without committing.

Distinct from:

- `LiqCalendar` — the standalone month grid with no surrounding chrome.
  Drop it inline anywhere you want a permanently visible date picker.
- `LiqDatePicker` — the wheel-style inline picker exposed under the
  pickers category.

## Default

The default — an empty trigger with a placeholder. Tap the trigger to
open the popover; pick a day to commit the selection.
