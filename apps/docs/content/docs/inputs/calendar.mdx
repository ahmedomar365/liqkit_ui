`LiqCalendar` is the iOS 26 month-grid calendar — a 7-column grid of
date cells with weekday headers, prev/next month controls, and a
month/year title. It is a top-level component you can drop anywhere
without the chrome that wraps `LiqDatePicker`.

## Default

The calendar in its default state. Tap a day to select it; the
focused month follows the selection. The month/year title sits to the
left, with chevrons on the right to step forward and back.

## With bounds

Pass `firstDate` and `lastDate` to constrain the selectable range.
Days outside the bounds render in a dim gray and are non-tappable;
chevrons still work so the grid can scroll past the bounds for
context.
