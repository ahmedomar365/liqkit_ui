`LiqStatusBar` is the iOS 26 phone status bar. It renders the clock label on
the left and signal bars, Wi-Fi glyph, and battery indicator on the right.
Control the foreground color with `brightness` — `Brightness.light` for dark
glyphs on a light surface, `Brightness.dark` for light glyphs on a dark surface.

## Light

The default variant with dark glyphs on a light background.
