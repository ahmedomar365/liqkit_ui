`LiqSkeleton` is the iOS 26 shimmer placeholder used while async
content loads. It animates a soft highlight sliding left-to-right
across a tinted base, in a rectangle, circle, or text bar shape.
`LiqSkeletonText` stacks multiple text bars into a paragraph-shaped
block.

## Rectangle

Pass `width` and `height` to render a rounded rectangle. The default
corner radius is 6pt; override with `borderRadius`.

<CodeSnippet snippet={skeletonRectSnippet} />

## Circle

Pass `shape: LiqSkeletonShape.circle` and a `width` to render a
circular placeholder. Circles use `width` for both axes and ignore
`height` and `borderRadius`.

<LiqPreview component="skeleton" variant="circle" />

<CodeSnippet snippet={skeletonCircleSnippet} />

## Text

`LiqSkeletonText` renders multiple text bars stacked into a
paragraph-shaped block. The final bar is shrunk to read like real
wrapped text; tune the shape with `lines`, `lineHeight`,
`lineSpacing`, and `lastLineWidthFraction`.
