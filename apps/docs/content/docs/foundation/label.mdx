`LiqLabel` is the iOS 26 form-field caption — a small, semibold, slightly
tinted line of text rendered above an input such as `LiqTextField` or
`LiqCheckbox`. `LiqFormField` composes a `LiqLabel`, the input control,
and an optional helper or error line into a vertical stack.

## Required

Pass `required: true` to append a red asterisk after the label. Helper
text is shown beneath the field in iOS secondary-label grey.

## Optional

Pass `optional: true` to append a muted " (optional)" suffix in regular
weight. The two flags are mutually exclusive.
