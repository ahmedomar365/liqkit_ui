`LiqDivider` is the iOS 26 hairline rule. It paints at the platform
physical thickness of 0.33pt in 16% black on white, fills its parent
along the long axis, and accepts symmetric `indent` / `endIndent`
insets. `LiqLabeledDivider` lays out two hairlines flanking a
centered text label such as "OR".

## Horizontal

The default orientation. The hairline stretches to fill its parent's
width; `indent` insets the leading edge and `endIndent` the trailing
edge.

## Vertical

Pass `LiqDividerOrientation.vertical` to render a 0.33pt-wide rule
that fills its parent's height. The parent must constrain the
divider's height — wrap it in a fixed-height container or place it
inside an `IntrinsicHeight`.

## With label

`LiqLabeledDivider` centers a text label between two hairlines. The
default style is SF 13pt in iOS secondary-label grey; pass
`textStyle` to override.
